# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Z²SE aims to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful project. Contributors and users are expected to communicate professionally, focus on the technical issue at hand, and treat others with respect regardless of experience level or background.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving clear, constructive feedback;
- helping reproduce and diagnose problems;
- respecting different technical approaches;
- keeping discussions focused and useful;
- avoiding harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory comments, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or abusive conduct are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these standards, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, use the repository's normal contact channels without posting sensitive personal information publicly. Security-sensitive matters should follow `SECURITY.md`.
